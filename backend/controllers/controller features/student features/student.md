Based on your **Student schema**, here are the features the **Student Management App** should include for students:

### **1. Authentication & Authorization**
- **Student Registration:** Students can sign up by providing their name, email, password, contact, and address.
- **Login System:** Secure login with email and password.
- **Password Management:** Change/reset password feature with email verification.
- **Role-Based Access:** Ensure students only access student-specific features.

### **2. Profile Management**
- **View Profile:** Students can view their personal details (name, email, contact, address, role).
- **Update Profile:** Ability to update contact, address, and profile picture.
- **Profile Picture Upload:** Upload and update a profile image stored in **AWS S3**.

### **3. Course Management**
- **View Enrolled Courses:** Students can see the courses they are enrolled in.
- **Enroll in a Course:** Option to enroll in available courses.
- **Drop a Course:** Request to withdraw from a course.

### **4. Attendance Tracking**
- **View Attendance Record:** Students can see their attendance for each course.
- **Attendance Notifications:** Get alerts for low attendance.

### **5. Score & Performance Tracking**
- **View Scores:** Students can check their scores/grades for different subjects.
- **Performance Insights:** Graphs or charts for progress tracking (if added later).

### **6. Notifications & Alerts**
- **Course Updates:** Notifications for new assignments, exams, and announcements.
- **Attendance Alerts:** Low attendance warnings.
- **Score Alerts:** Notifications when new scores are uploaded.

### **7. Communication & Support**
- **Messaging System:** Chat with instructors/admins (optional).
- **Help & Support:** Raise queries regarding courses, attendance, or other issues.

### **8. Security & Privacy**
- **Two-Factor Authentication (Optional):** Extra security layer.
- **Data Privacy Settings:** Students can manage their visibility settings.
